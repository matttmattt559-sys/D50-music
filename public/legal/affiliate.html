Creator partners · Draft for Denmark

Affiliate Rules

Before publication: identify the D50 operator and contact email. Confirm in a separate agreement whether partners receive money or another benefit, how any payout is calculated and when it is made.

Make advertising clear

If you receive money, free access, a discount or another benefit for promoting D50, clearly identify each relevant post or video as advertising at the beginning. For Danish audiences, use a prominent label such as “Reklame for D50 Music” or “Annonce for D50 Music”. A code, tag, platform badge or a disclosure hidden at the end may not be enough. Disclose the relationship even when you share your code on YouTube or another platform.

Describe the offer accurately

Tell viewers the current price and terms shown in D50's checkout. A creator code currently reduces the one-time 30-day Premium pass from 36.00 DKK to 28.80 DKK. Do not call it a recurring subscription, claim guaranteed earnings, promise rights to music or imply that D50 has endorsed an artist without permission.

Referral count and conduct

Approval gives you a D50 code and a referral count for eligible paid purchases. A referral count alone is not a payout promise. Do not mislead viewers, create fake purchases or use someone else's music or likeness in promotions without permission. D50 may suspend a code for abuse; material commercial terms belong in the partner agreement.

Commission estimate

D50 currently tracks an estimated commission of 10% of each eligible, completed 28.80 DKK Premium code purchase (2.88 DKK per full-price code sale). Refunds reduce this estimate proportionally. The displayed balance has not been paid, and automatic payouts are not available. D50 and each partner must agree to payment eligibility, timing, tax documentation and payout details before money is disbursed. Historical referrals made before commission tracking may not appear in the estimate.
