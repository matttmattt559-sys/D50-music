Privacy Notice

Before publication: add the data controller's legal name, postal address, privacy contact email, effective date, actual retention periods, lawful bases and any international transfer details. Review the real service and processor agreements before calling this notice final.

Data D50 handles

To run accounts we process email addresses, password hashes, session identifiers, account status and access settings. To provide the music service we process uploaded tracks and cover images, titles, uploader identity, likes, categories, listening counts and moderation reports. For purchases and creator referrals we process access dates, amounts or plan status, Stripe payment or checkout references, and creator-code usage. D50's Stripe integration uses payment references; card details are entered into Stripe's checkout, not D50's song database.

Why and with whom

We use account and activity data to provide login, playback limits, libraries, moderation, customer support, payments, fraud prevention and legal compliance. D50 uses Render for hosting and its database, Cloudflare R2 for media storage and delivery, and Stripe for payments. Public songs and covers are accessible through R2's public media URLs. We do not publish your password hash or payment reference as part of a song.

How long, and your choices

We keep account and activity data only for the periods needed for the stated purposes and applicable legal obligations; specific periods must be inserted in the final policy. You may ask for access, correction or deletion, subject to legal retention requirements. You may also have rights to restriction, objection and data portability. Contact the controller above to make a request. You can complain to Datatilsynet.

Sessions and security

D50 uses an essential login cookie to keep your session active. Do not treat the absence of an advertising cookie banner as a substitute for checking all analytics and third-party scripts actually deployed. Access is restricted by account role, but no online service can promise absolute security. Contact the controller if you suspect unauthorised account access.
